WHAT'S THE CATCH

Why Is It Free?

There's no catch.  We've found most parents prefer a free training to feel things out.  See if we know what we're doing, and if we can actually help your Athlete level up.

And we want to know if you're a good fit for us too! It doesn't help us or you if you're in the wrong program.  A short evaluation is the simplest way to make sure we get your player the support they need.  If we believe we can help, we'll make you an offer for a specific training program.  Either way you leave knowing where your athlete stands.

Who this is for

Experienced Players Only

This is for players with two plus years of competitive experience, goals of playing Varsity, College and Pro, who are looking for more individualized training to take their games to the next level.

If your athlete is new to the game, only played Jazz, starting their first year of Bantam, we'd recommend the come to Group Training.  If they're young, Kindergarten-2nd grade and looking to get started, we'd recommend our Rookie Group Class.

HOW CAN TRAINING ZONE HELP

Our Training Programs

Our training programs are designed for elite players, or players who've shown the drive and dedication to become elite.  These are highly individual programs, tailored to specific needs.  Our 90 Day Shot Program for elite shooters, 90 Day Player Dev for scorers and play makers, and our Academy for players who want to double their time in the gym.  The goal of the Free Training, is to figure out your players needs, and which training programs can help them most.

WHY TRAINING ZONE

Where Elite Players Come To Work

Training Zone is home to some of Utah's finest ball players.  You'll regularly find College and Pro players, top High school Players, and young rising starts grinding and building their game.  Our trainers have Pro and College experience, worked and trained under the most recognized trainers in the game, and have over 20 years combined experience training athletes.  This is where Elite ball players come to work.

Quick answers

Frequently asked.

Is this really free?

Yes. No cost, no obligation. We're betting you'll want more once you see it — but that's on us to earn.

What should my athlete bring?

Their gear for their sport, water, and the willingness to work. We handle the rest.

How long is it?

About fifteen minutes — your athlete works, then we spend a few minutes with you on what we saw.

My kid is young / advanced / brand new — is that okay?

Yes. The session meets them where they are — that's the whole point of one-on-one.

Do you offer Soccer and Volleyball too?

We do — both are running this year alongside Basketball. Book a session in your athlete's sport.

What happens after?

If it's a fit, we'll talk about the right program to keep the momentum going. If it's not, no pressure at all.
