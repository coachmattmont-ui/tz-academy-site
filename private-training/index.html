The Program

A ROADMAP,
NOT AN HOUR.

A good trainer gives a player accountability, teaching, and insight. But what actually moves an athlete is what they do on the days they aren't with a trainer — so we write them a program, using YourStats, and track their progress.

1

WE EVALUATE

Every player starts with an evaluation. We need to know exactly where your athlete is before we write a single thing.

2

WE BUILD THE PROGRAM

Their trainer builds a program around what your athlete actually needs — not a template, and not whatever the player next to them is doing.

3

WE TRAIN

Four coached sessions a month working that program. Part of every session is teaching the concepts and drills they'll work on their own that week — your athlete leaves knowing exactly what to do.

4

WE TEST AND ADJUST

Week by week the trainer adjusts the program and re-tests to verify the growth is real. Everything is tracked in YourStats, so nobody is guessing.

As a bonus, players also get 24/7 gym access, every group training we run, and shot feedback — use one of your four sessions as a shooting session, then send film any time and we'll break down your shot.
For full shot development, see our 90 Day Shot Program.

Who's Coaching My Athlete?

IT TAKES
A VILLAGE.

What separates our training from other programs is that every trainer you work with has eyes on your goals and development. We built our own software, called YourStats, to coordinate your athlete's training across different coaches and maximize their growth.

01

A LEAD
TRAINER

Your lead trainer is your director of player development. They monitor and oversee your growth, and coordinate with every one of your trainers to make sure you're moving forward.

02

EXTRA
HANDS

You'll work with multiple coaches through this process, always under the coordination of your lead. Every coach will know your goals, your areas of focus, your assigned workouts, and your current evaluations.

03

YOURSTATS

A tool we built to help lead trainers oversee the training and development of our elite players. It's how we make sure that whoever your athlete is working with, they're always on point and moving toward their goals.

QUESTIONS.

What ages do you train?

We work with players across elementary, junior high, and high school. Sessions are built around the individual player, so the age matters a lot less than where they are in their development.

My athlete is brand new to skill training. Is that a problem?

Not at all. One-on-one is the best possible place to start — there's nowhere to hide and nothing to keep up with. The plan meets them exactly where they are.

How is this different from just booking private sessions?

Sessions alone are a couple of hours a month. The membership adds the part that actually compounds: a custom program your athlete works all week, adjusted and re-tested as they go, plus film feedback on their shot whenever they send video, group reps, and gym access.

Can we do more than the included sessions?

Yes — members can add sessions at a member rate that's well below what non-members pay. Ask us and we'll walk you through it.

Do you train sports other than basketball?

Private Training is basketball. Soccer and volleyball programming launches at Training Zone in October 2026.

How do we get started?

Book a free evaluation. It takes about fifteen minutes — your athlete works, we tell you what we see in their game, and we talk about whether the membership makes sense. No pressure either way.
