WHAT IT'S GOOD FOR.

This is a big, clean, open space rather than a banquet hall — which makes it a good fit for groups who want room to do something, and a poor fit for anyone who wants a venue that runs the event for them.

GOOD FITS

  • ✓Corporate events, sales nights and awards banquets
  • ✓School dances and youth group nights
  • ✓Quinceañeras and large family celebrations
  • ✓Congregations without a building of their own
  • ✓Family reunions
  • ✓Vendor markets and small trade shows
  • ✓Camps, clinics and fitness groups

NOT A FIT

  • ✕Kids' birthday parties
  • ✕Hourly or drop-in court time — that's court rentals
  • ✕Events needing a venue to plan and staff them
  • ✕Anything requiring a commercial kitchen on site

We rent you the space. We don't plan the event, cater it, or decorate it — you bring your own people for that, and you're free to bring in whoever you like.

WHAT'S HERE, AND WHAT ISN'T.

Being straight about this up front saves everyone a walkthrough. If it matters to your event, ask on the call and we'll tell you exactly where things stand.

WHAT YOU GET

  • ✓The full building, private to your group, 3–10pm
  • ✓Both courts and the open floor
  • ✓Restrooms
  • ✓Free parking on site
  • ✓A floor that can be used — run a game, a tournament, a shooting contest

WHAT YOU BRING

  • •Tables and chairs for seated setups — we don't stock event tables yet
  • •Food and drink. There's no kitchen here; groups have brought catering, or worked a food truck into the lot
  • •Decor, and anyone you need to run the night

Groups have made all of this work. Tell us what you're planning on the call and we'll tell you honestly whether the room fits it.

PRICING.

$2,800 – $5,500  / 3–10pm buyout

Where you land in that range depends on the day. Weekdays sit at the lower end; Saturdays, Sundays and holidays at the top. Tell us your date and we'll quote it exactly — no packages to decode.

COME WALK THE FLOOR.

Easiest way to know whether it works for your event is to stand in it. Call or text and we'll set up a time — most groups book about a month out.
