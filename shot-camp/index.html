WHAT WE DO EACH NIGHT.

Shooting is a lifelong build. Nobody becomes an elite shooter in three days — but three days is enough to put the right foundation under a player and hand them the plan to keep going.

Night 1
The three pillars of shooting — balance, alignment and timing. We find where your shot actually is and what has to change.
Night 2
Build on that foundation while working game reps, so the new mechanics hold up at speed.
Night 3
Catch and shoot threes, and how to extend range without breaking the mechanics you just built.
